Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Millie is made by LLMs For All, Inc. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Millie app. The short version: Millie runs entirely on your device, your conversations never leave it, and the only thing we collect is anonymous information about how the app is used — never what you say to Millie.

Your conversations never leave your device

This is the part that does not change:

  • Everything you type, and everything Millie replies, stays on your device. It is processed by an AI model running on the device itself. Your messages are never sent to us, and we have no way to read them — unless you choose to send them to us yourself, as described under "If you send us feedback" below.
  • Photos you share with Millie stay on your device. They are read by the model locally and are not uploaded.
  • No accounts. You do not sign up or log in to use Millie, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location.

Anonymous usage analytics

To understand whether Millie is actually useful and where it falls short, we collect limited, anonymous information about how the app is used — which buttons and screens people use, and when the app is opened — along with basic technical details: whether you are on iOS or Android, your operating system version, and the app version.

So that we can tell one installation's activity apart from another's, the app creates a random identifier the first time it runs and sends it alongside this information. It is not derived from your device or from anything about you, it is not shared with anyone, and deleting the app discards it.

This information:

  • never includes the content of your conversations, your messages, Millie's replies, or any photos you share;
  • is not linked to your name, email, or any account, because there is no account; and
  • is used only to improve the app.

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

If you send us feedback

The app has a Feedback option for telling us what is wrong or what could be better. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and you can always see exactly what will be sent before you send it.

What reaches us is what you wrote, together with a few technical details: the model name and version, and your platform and operating system version. Your conversation is not included unless you explicitly choose to attach it.

Feedback reaches our support inbox, where we read and keep it in order to reply to you and to fix problems with the app. If you write to us by email, we will also have your email address, because that is how the message arrives; if you send feedback from inside the app, we do not receive your address unless you include it yourself. You can reach us directly at support@llmsforall.com.

Downloading the model

Millie needs an AI model to work, and that model is too large to include in the app download. The first time you open Millie — and later, if you choose to accept an improved model — the app downloads the model from our content delivery network at models.llmsforall.com.

As with any download from any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes the technical information required to deliver the file, such as your IP address, and may keep short-lived operational logs. We do not use this information to identify you, we do not combine it with anything else, and it is never linked to your conversations, which we never see.

Apart from that download and any anonymous analytics described above, Millie makes no network connections. Chatting with Millie works fully offline.

Data you can delete

Your conversations are stored only on your device. You can delete individual chats in the app. Uninstalling Millie, or clearing the app's storage in your device settings, removes everything: your chats and the downloaded model.

Children

Millie is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

AI-generated content

Millie's replies are generated by an AI model and may be inaccurate. Please do not rely on them for medical, legal, financial, or other important decisions without checking with a qualified professional.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If we ever make a change that affects how your information is handled, we will say so clearly in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: support@llmsforall.com
