How can we help?

Email support@llmsforall.com and a person will read it. Below are answers to the questions we are asked most.

Millie for iPhone

Millie is a private AI chat app. The model runs entirely on your phone, so your conversations never leave the device and never reach a server. Once it is set up, it works with no internet connection.

Millie says my phone is not supported

Millie needs an iPhone with at least 6 GB of memory, because the whole AI model has to be held in memory while you chat. That includes iPhone 14 and later, and the Pro models of iPhone 12 and iPhone 13. On a phone with less memory Millie will tell you when you first open it, rather than downloading a large file that cannot run.

There is no setting or workaround for this. A smaller phone would become unresponsive rather than simply running slowly.

The download is slow, or it stops partway

The first launch downloads about 2.3 GB. Stay on Wi-Fi and keep the app open while it runs. If it does stop, nothing is lost: reopening Millie continues from where it left off rather than starting again.

You also need free space for it. If your phone is nearly full, Millie will say how much room it needs.

Where are my conversations stored?

Only on your phone. They are never uploaded, and we have no way to read them. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

How do I delete everything?

Delete individual chats inside the app, or uninstall Millie to remove everything at once, including the downloaded model.

Millie got something wrong

Millie runs on a small model that fits on a phone, so it is less capable than cloud AI, and it is weakest on niche or recent facts. Please check anything important. If a mistake seems worth reporting, use Send Feedback in Settings, or email us.
