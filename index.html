Models and software

Millie

Capable language models. Small enough to take with you.

Open weights · Text and image input

We trained Millie to retain useful coding and agent capabilities at very low weight precision. These 35-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts models activate about 3 billion parameters per token, with releases of approximately 7, 9, and 11 GB including vision.

Our custom weight formats and inference kernels make these models practical across phones, laptops, and desktops. Millie builds on Agents-A1 and the Qwen3.5 architecture.
