01.MILLIE MODELS

Capable models, compressed for phones and laptops

35BPARAMETERS

~3BACTIVE / TOKEN

We train highly compressed models for local reasoning, conversation, and coding. Our 35-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts models use roughly 3 billion parameters per token. Millie 1.1 brings our ternary model to phones; the original Millie family powers our coding agent.

We build the weight formats, inference kernels, and memory management that make these models practical across phones, laptops, and desktops.
